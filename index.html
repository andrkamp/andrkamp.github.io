The chocolate cookie is very popular but it was created with an easy idea: what happens if you combine chocolate with a cookie. The most famous chocolate cookie was made by Ruth Graves in 1930s Wakefield. She also owned the Toll House Inn in Massachusetts. Ruth decided to add pieces of chocolate in cookies. She thought the chocolate would melt but instead the chocolate kept its shape. That's how she made the chocolate cookie. The cookie had butter sugar, eggs, flour, and other ingredients. Soon it was published in the news. You can add nuts and other things and now it is enjoyed by many.

how elictricity was invented

Long ago, people noticed static electricity when materials were rubbed together. In the 1700s scientists started studying electricity more carefully. Benjamin Franklin demonstrated that lightning was electrical with a kite experiment. A major breakthrough came in 1800 when Alessandro Volta created the first battery. Scientists discovered that electricity could also be produced using magnets and motion. In 1831, Michael Faraday demonstrated electromagnetic induction, showing that moving a magnet through a coil of wire could generate electricity. This discovery became the foundation of many modern generators. During the late 1800s, inventors such as Thomas Edison and Nikola Tesla helped develop electrical systems. Power stations began to produce large amounts of electricity. Electricity could then travel through wires to homes, businesses, and factories. Even though the technology has changed greatly, the basic goal remains the same: converting another form of energy into electrical energy that people can use.
